USING THIS SITE

Terms of use

These terms describe the current calculator. Last reviewed 4 November 2026.

Every result is an estimate for planning. It is not an official tax assessment or a payslip. Confirm actual payroll figures with your employer, and consult the Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA) or a qualified tax adviser about your own circumstances.

What the calculator covers

The calculator estimates monthly PAYE, selected statutory contributions and take-home pay for a resident employee using the payroll month and amounts you enter. The historical PAYE view supports October 2009 through September 2024 and estimates PAYE only. The full payslip view supports October 2024 through October 2026. The raise, contribution and loan tools are planning illustrations. Annual figures on the salary breakdown are monthly estimates multiplied by 12; they are not a separate annual tax calculation.

The independent consultant view is a separate illustration for a resident individual using 2026 rules. It shows possible withholding on a client payment and a simplified annual income tax estimate from the figures you enter. It does not calculate VAT, SHIF, Housing Levy, pension, instalment tax, other income, losses or a company's tax. Withholding on resident professional fees is usually a credit toward annual tax, not the final tax. Your client and KRA records determine the actual amounts.

The calculator does not file a return, pay tax, issue a tax determination or replace your employer's payroll process. Its assumptions and official rule sources are shown on the calculator page.

Why your actual figures may differ

Your employer's calculation may include other taxable non-cash benefits, exempt amounts, pension scheme conditions, other income or adjustments that this tool does not model. A deduction, relief or exemption may depend on eligibility, the statutory limit and documents you give your employer. Your inputs, your employer's payroll records, rounding and later rule changes can also affect the final amount.

KRA says employers calculate and remit PAYE and must apply relevant deductions, reliefs and exemptions where the conditions and supporting documents are met. See KRA's PAYE guidance and employer guidance on deductions and reliefs.

Unused deductions and reliefs

The unused-allowance figures compare entered eligible payments with the selected month’s limits. They do not establish eligibility, recommend a product, promise a refund or instruct you to make a payment. Each estimated PAYE reduction changes one payment while holding the other inputs fixed. The individual savings must not be added together. Extra payments can exceed the tax saved, and pension top-ups may increase the taxable employer contribution.

Use actual qualifying payments and keep supporting documents. Past-month allowances cannot be used by making a payment today. Annual illustrations multiply a monthly result by 12 and do not establish a full-year entitlement. Consultant expenses have no general allowance to fill; statutory contributions and personal relief are not voluntary tax-saving targets. Certificate-based exemptions and unmodeled claims need individual review.

Checking and using a result

Review the selected month, your inputs and the displayed assumptions before using a result. For your actual pay, ask your employer's payroll team to explain the payslip. For a tax obligation, return or circumstance-specific question, consult KRA's taxpayer support or a qualified tax adviser. Do not use this estimate as the sole figure for filing or paying tax.

Accuracy and availability

The linked government publications support the rules implemented for the displayed months. Rules and official guidance may change. The review date above records the latest review of these terms; it is not a promise of continuous updates or uninterrupted availability. If a result seems wrong, verify it against current official guidance and your payroll records.

Independence and external sites

This is an independent calculator. It is not operated, approved or endorsed by KRA or another Kenyan government body. Links to official websites are provided so you can check source material. Those websites have their own terms and privacy practices.

Privacy and operator details

The current calculator processes the salary figures you enter in your browser tab. See the privacy notice for details about site visits and data handling. The public site is hosted on GitHub Pages. The website operator’s name and contact route have not yet been supplied; those details remain pending.
