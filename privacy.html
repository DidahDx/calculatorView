YOUR DATA

Privacy notice

This notice describes the current calculator. Last reviewed 6 November 2026.

Your pay and consulting figures are calculated in your browser. The calculator does not send them to a server or save them in browser storage.

Pay, consulting and tax inputs

The amounts and payroll month you enter are used to show PAYE, statutory contributions, estimated take-home pay, or a consulting tax illustration, loan planning and unused-allowance comparisons. They remain in this browser tab while you use the calculator. Reloading or closing the page clears them. The current app has no account, submission form, pay database, analytics, advertising scripts, or browser storage for these inputs.

Website visits and links

Your browser requests the website files from the server that hosts the calculator. A hosting provider may process technical information such as your IP address, browser details, requested pages and request times to deliver and secure the site. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub states that Pages visitors’ IP addresses are logged and stored for security, even without signing in. See GitHub Pages data collection.

GitHub’s privacy statement describes its service providers, international processing and retention practices. Processing can take place outside Kenya, including in the United States. A site-specific hosting-log retention period has not been confirmed. These hosting requests do not include the financial amounts entered in this calculator.

The calculator links to official sources. If you choose to open one, that website may process information about your visit under its own privacy terms. Links that open a new tab suppress referrer information.

Appearance preference

Choosing Light or Dark saves only that preference in your browser's local storage under kenya-paye-calculator:theme. It stays on this browser until you choose System or clear the site's browser data. System follows your device's appearance setting. The preference is shared by this site's calculator and policy pages and is not sent to a server. If browser storage is unavailable, the choice works for the current page only. No pay, tax, pension, insurance or loan inputs are included in this preference.

Cookies and other tracking

The current calculator code does not set cookies, load analytics or advertising tags, or use remote fonts. This notice must be updated before any of those features are added.

Your choices and rights

Reload or close the page to clear pay and consulting figures from browser memory. Reset to example keeps one temporary undo copy until your next edit or reload. Because the app does not persist those figures, it cannot retrieve or delete a past calculation. Kenya’s data protection law provides rights concerning personal data held by an operator or its hosting provider, including rights to information, access, correction, objection and deletion where applicable. Read the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner’s explanation of your rights.

Operator and contact details

The website operator’s name and public privacy contact have not yet been supplied. These details remain pending for this public site. You can also learn about making a complaint on the ODPC website.

Changes to this notice

Review and update this page whenever the calculator’s data flows or hosting arrangement change. The review date above should change only after that review happens.
