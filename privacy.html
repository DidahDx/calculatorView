YOUR DATA

Privacy notice

This notice describes the current calculator preview. Last reviewed 29 October 2026.

Your pay and consulting figures are calculated in your browser. The calculator does not send them to a server or save them in browser storage.

Pay, consulting and tax inputs

The amounts and payroll month you enter are used to show PAYE, statutory contributions, estimated take-home pay, or a consulting tax illustration. They remain in this browser tab while you use the calculator. Reloading or closing the page clears them. The current app has no account, submission form, pay database, analytics, advertising scripts, or browser storage for these inputs.

Website visits and links

Your browser requests the website files from the server that hosts the calculator. A hosting provider may process technical information such as your IP address, browser details, requested pages and request times to deliver and secure the site. The operator, provider, log retention period and processing location have not yet been set for a public deployment and must be added to this notice before launch.

The calculator links to official sources. If you choose to open one, that website may process information about your visit under its own privacy terms. Source links open with referrer information suppressed.

Cookies and other tracking

The current calculator code does not set cookies, load analytics or advertising tags, or use remote fonts. This notice must be updated before any of those features are added.

Your choices and rights

Use the relevant reset button or reload the page to clear pay and consulting figures from the current tab. Because the app does not store those figures, it cannot retrieve or delete a past calculation. Kenya’s data protection law provides rights concerning personal data held by an operator or its hosting provider, including rights to information, access, correction, objection and deletion where applicable. Read the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner’s explanation of your rights.

Operator and contact details

This is a local project preview. The website operator and a privacy contact have not yet been supplied. Those details, along with hosting information, must be published here before the site is made public. You can also learn about making a complaint on the ODPC website.

Changes to this notice

Review and update this page whenever the calculator’s data flows or hosting arrangement change. The review date above should change only after that review happens.
